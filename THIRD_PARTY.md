# Third-party components

E-DynVLA contains adapted portions of the following projects because they are
required to reproduce the simulation and model pipeline.

## DynamicVLA

- Source: https://github.com/hzxie/DynamicVLA
- Retained in: `E-DynVLA/policies/dynamicvla`, selected simulation, training,
  inference, and utility files
- License: `E-DynVLA/LICENSE`

## EVIS / Isaac Sim event-camera plugin

- Source: https://github.com/spikelab-jhu/isaac-sim-event-camera-plugin
- Retained in: `V2E-VLA/dvs_gen`, with project-specific v2/v3/v4 and motion
  separation changes
- License: `V2E-VLA/LICENSE`

The original repositories' unrelated examples, media, assets, and utilities
are not included. Upstream authorship is not claimed as original E-DynVLA
work.
